# Shadow Warriors: ten-guardian revision

## Candid audit
The previous campaign inflated content count with repeated encounter recipes.
Different IDs, HP totals and scene permutations did not amount to 100 authored
stories. Four AI styles lacked enough readable counterplay. The attractive camp
art promised more polish than the procedural combat delivered. Automated green
checks established deterministic outcomes, not enjoyment or competition quality.

## Submission candidate direction
Ten named antagonists, ten connected consequences, ten encounter rules. The
original Beacon plot connects mercy, complicity, prediction, memory and control.
Both protagonists act in scenes; defeated guardians make consequential choices.
The final objective is preserving the voices while severing the command network.

Each fight displays a counterplay instruction. Boss wind-ups last at least half
a second and expose recovery time. Shared cutscene skips remain consent-based.
The story route skips procedural filler. Legacy saves advance to their next
chapter guardian without removing upgrades or completed-campaign state.

## Implementation and visual limits
Scenes use ten authored silhouette tableaux and a shared skeletal drawing rig,
with distinct props, motions and dialogue. They are not ten cinematic movies.
Combat remains a small procedural 2D fighter, not a commercial-scale animation
production. Four base movement archetypes support the ten added encounter rules.
No claim of top-ten ranking, guaranteed fun or competition victory is justified.

## Release gates
- Deterministic replay, JSON restore and delayed/reordered input convergence.
- Tests for every guardian rule and its intended counter.
- Progress migration, duplicate victory protection and ending persistence.
- Two independent browser contexts, host/guest inputs and shared story gates.
- Keyboard, touch emulation, 20 viewport sizes and rotation.
- Physical laptop-plus-phone play remains a required owner acceptance test.
- Do not submit until the owner has played and approved the candidate.

## Remaining product limitations
Local names and saves only; no accounts, friend list or cloud restoration.
A disconnected player must create/join a new room. Free hosting can sleep or
restart. Browser viewport tests do not certify all phones, GPUs or Safari.
Bots cannot establish whether the story is emotionally effective or a fight is
fun. Human feedback is required before calling this competition-ready.
